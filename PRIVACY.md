# Privacy Policy — Talking Tags

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Talking Tags (`com.appricodes.voicetag`) is made by Mohammad Amiri Moalla, an individual developer
(see the [Imprint](#imprint) below). This policy explains what the app does with your data. In
short: **the app collects nothing, sends nothing, and has no internet access.**

## What the app does not do

- It has **no internet permission**, so it cannot send anything anywhere by itself.
- No accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no crash reporting.
- It does not keep a history of the tags you read.

## Data on your NFC tags

When you write to a tag, the app stores on the tag itself: the text you entered, the date and
time it was written, and, if you recorded a voice note, a random number that links the tag to
that note. The text is stored in the standard NFC text format, so **anyone who holds the tag to
an NFC phone can read it**. Do not put secrets on a tag. You can erase a tag in the app, or lock
it so it can no longer be changed.

## Voice notes

- Recorded only when you press *Record voice note*, using the microphone permission you grant.
  The microphone is not used at any other time.
- Stored only in the app's private storage on your phone.
- Excluded from Google's cloud backup. They are copied when you transfer your data directly to a
  new phone.
- *Export voice notes* writes a ZIP file to a place you choose (for example *Downloads*). From
  there, it is under your control and visible to apps that can read that folder.
- Uninstalling the app deletes them.

## Services provided by your phone

The app uses parts of Android that may be supplied by other companies, under their own privacy
policies:

- **Dictation** uses your phone's speech-recognition service (often Google's). The audio you
  speak is processed by that service, possibly online.
- **Reading aloud** uses the text-to-speech engine you choose in Settings, or TalkBack. The text
  being spoken is passed to that engine.
- **Tags from other apps** (web links, phone numbers, email addresses, Wi-Fi networks, contacts…)
  are only shown to you. Another app — your browser, dialer, mail app, Wi-Fi settings, contacts
  app — is opened only when you press the matching button.

## Settings

Your settings (speech output, dictation language, vibration, automatic playback) are stored on
your phone only.

## Children

The app collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

If this policy changes, the new version is published at this address with a new date.
